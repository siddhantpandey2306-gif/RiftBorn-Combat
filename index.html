💥 HEAVY ATTACK
[HEAVY] or K

High-damage launcher and finisher strike. Branches from Light combo or hits raw.

🛡 BLOCK DEFENSE
[BLOCK] or SHIFT

Hold to activate kinetic barrier. Mitigates 85% of incoming attack damage.

💨 DODGE ROLL
[DODGE] or SPACE

Invulnerable evasive roll. Evades through enemy strikes and recovers into counter-attacks.

⚡ SPECIAL ABILITIES
[SPECIAL] or L / U / I

Character-specific signature abilities. Consumes 25-30 Core Energy upon execution.

[ULTIMATE] or I / O

Screen-cleaving super execution. Requires 100% Core Energy. Cancels out of launchers and specials.

⚔ COUNTER PARRY
H
