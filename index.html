PRIVACY POLICY

LAST UPDATED: OCTOBER 17, 2026

1. Zero External Telemetry & Tracking

RIFTBORN is an open-source browser-based fighting game designed with client-first privacy architecture. We do not use remote tracking pixels, third-party analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics or Meta Pixel), advertising trackers, or device fingerprinting. Your gameplay interactions, combative inputs, and browsing habits remain entirely private inside your local browser sandbox.

2. What Information Is Stored & Why

All gameplay progress and custom preferences are stored strictly on your device using HTML5 localStorage. We collect zero personally identifiable information (no real names, physical addresses, telephone numbers, emails, or government identification).

Storage Key Classification Technical Purpose
riftborn_save_data Essential Gameplay Maintains your operative tag, character unlocks, level mastery, Rift Shards, Cosmetic Prisms, and competitive ladder RP.
riftborn_theme UI Preference Remembers your visual theme choice (light or dark mode).
riftborn_touch_config Ergonomics Saves custom on-screen virtual joystick and button coordinates for mobile gameplay.
riftborn_tutorial_completed UX Flag Records whether you have finished the introductory training sequence.
riftborn_consent Compliance Stores your acknowledgment of our privacy & storage transparency notice.

How does the Kinetic Block & Parry counter system work?
Holding SHIFT (or the touch [BLOCK] button) activates continuous Kinetic Block, mitigating 85% of incoming strike damage with a slight chip damage penalty. Tapping H (or [PARRY]) right before an enemy strike connects executes a Perfect Parry: this fully negates damage, stuns the attacker for 28 frames, and delivers guaranteed counter damage.
How are Energy and Core Ultimate gauge charged?
Every clean Light or Heavy strike landed on the opponent builds your Core Energy bar. Successfully blocking attacks or dodging also grants defensive energy bonuses. Once Core reaches 100%, tap I (or touch [ULTIMATE]) to execute your character's cinematic finishing move.
Is an internet connection or backend database required?
No. RIFTBORN runs 100% locally in your modern web browser using client-side HTML5 Canvas and Web Audio synthesis. All unlocks, character mastery, and difficulty preferences persist in your browser's local storage. Real multiplayer mode can optionally connect to an active WebSocket server when configured.
How do Seasonal Ladder and Elo/RP rankings work?
Matches won in Ranked 1v1 award Rating Points (RP) based on performance and opponent difficulty tier (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond). At season transition, a soft-reset formula retains 50% of earned placement progress and awards Rift Shards bonus rewards.
Can I customize mobile touch controls?
Yes! Open System Configuration > Mobile Touch HUD or launch the Touch Customizer directly from the guide. You can resize joystick clusters, reposition attack buttons, and adjust HUD opacity to match your phone's grip.
